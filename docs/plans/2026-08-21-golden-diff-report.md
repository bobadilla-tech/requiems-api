## Generator vs Hand-written — Sample Diff (Safe Endpoints)

Intentional differences between generated and hand-written snippets:

- **Response printing**: generator prints `response.json()["data"]` as a whole.
  Hand-written examples cherry-pick specific fields (e.g. `data["result"]`).
  This is a deliberate trade-off for robustness over readability.
- **No field-specific comments**: hand-written snippets often include inline
  comments (`# SGVsbG8...`). Generator omits these.

These differences are acceptable for regeneration purposes. Endpoints where the
generator output would be confusing to a first-time user are already in the
manual-override bucket.
